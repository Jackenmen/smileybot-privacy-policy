## Stored personal data

Red and the cogs included with it collect some amount of data about users for the bot's normal operations. This is mostly the ID your user is assigned by Discord, linked to a handful of things depending on what you interact with in the bot.

Depending on how server administrators configure the bot, Red may keep track of a short history of usernames/nicknames for moderation purposes.
The bot stores your last known username whenever server staff carries out a moderation action against you as part of its moderation log.
It will also remember which actions were taken using your Discord account (such as creating a music playlist) as well as the content of specific messages used directly as commands for the bot (such as reports sent to servers).

By default, Red will not collect any more data than it needs, and will not use it for anything other than the portion of the Red’s functionality that necessitated it.

Some of Red's plugins may store and use additional data that we've listed data statements for separately on the following page:
[Additional Data Statements](./3rd-party.html)

## Data retention

-   User IDs, last known username, and short history of usernames/nickname tied to moderation actions are stored indefinitely as an anti-abuse measure.
    When you delete your Discord account, you can request this data to be deleted as described in the "Data access and deletion requests" section below.
-   User IDs and any content you've provided us when creating resources using your Discord account (such as creating a music playlist or a custom command) are stored until that resource (music playlist, custom command, etc.) is deleted.

```

```
where `@mention` should be replaced with a mention of the bot.
